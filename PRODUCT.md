# PRODUCT.md — Solo Doctor Clinic Master Template A

## 1. Product Truth & Value Proposition
"We help independent doctors establish a credible online presence, attract potential patients and make appointment enquiries easier through a professional clinic website."

This codebase is a production-grade, reusable SiteForge Tier 2 Master Template (`clinic-standard`) designed for solo general physicians and family clinics across India.

- **Location:** Shop No. 12, Ground Floor, Shanti Vihar Commercial Complex, Ring Road, Indore, MP - 452001
- **Consultation Fee:** ₹400 Outpatient Consultation
- **Follow-up Policy:** 7-Day Complimentary Follow-Up (₹0 fee for same illness/report review)

## 3. Surface Modes
- **Persuade (Home & Services):** Single-screen commanding hero (1440x900 standard), doctor credibility card, live OPD status badge, transparent pricing card, and clear primary CTA.
- **Operate (Appointment Enquiry System):** Dual Web Enquiry Form + Direct WhatsApp pre-filled booking modal with instant confirmation token allocation.
- **Read (About Doctor, FAQs, Timings & Location, Existing Patients):** Honest clinical information, qualification proofs, parking & transit directions, and patient retention guidance.

## 4. Business Objectives & Guardrails
1. **Zero Medical Fabrication:** No exaggerated medical claims, fake cures, or unverified credentials.
2. **Dual Appointment Enquiry:** Form submissions generate reference tokens without false promises of automated instant confirmation; WhatsApp handoff opens a clean pre-filled draft.
3. **Fee Transparency:** Standard ₹400 outpatient fee and 7-day complimentary follow-up window clearly declared.
4. **Emergency Triage Notice:** Prominent guidance routing acute life-threatening emergencies to tertiary casualty/ICU departments.
